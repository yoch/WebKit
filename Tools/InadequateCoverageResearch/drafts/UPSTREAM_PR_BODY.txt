Historical draft below, retained only for reference:

Reoptimize sooner on repeated same-site InadequateCoverage OSR exits.

Downstream: https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/issues/40477
Reproducer: https://github.com/yoch/frozenminisearch/blob/61d4ee365fd31369bd07fa89cf9d26c596926dfb/benchmarks/jsc/inadequate-coverage-repro.js

## What

DFG/FTL plant `ForceOSRExit` / `InadequateCoverage` for `SpecNone`
bytecode. That site then exits on every subsequent hit until recompile.
Today only the global `osrExitCountForReoptimization` (100) or
FromLoop (5) can jettison, so a newly hot uncovered site burns ~101
deopts.

This adds a dedicated per-site budget
`osrExitCountForReoptimizationFromInadequateCoverage` (default 5,
`m_count > threshold` after increment, plus the existing
`adjustedExitCountThreshold` backoff). FromLoop is not reused.
`codeTypeThresholdMultiplier()` is applied like the other helpers.

## What this is not

Not a correctness fix. Not a measured standalone-JSC wall-time win
on the Bun #40477 repro (FTL+concurrent, no tracing): 101→6 exits
are real; phase2 median across 64…200k updates is noise / slightly
slower at small N because the recompile sits in the window. Do not
cite verbose-OSR percentages.
