Reoptimize sooner on repeated same-site InadequateCoverage exits
https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=XXXXXX

Reviewed by NOBODY (OOPS!).

ForceOSRExit planted for SpecNone bytecode is deterministic: every
subsequent execution that reaches that ForceOSRExit site exits until
the CodeBlock is recompiled. The generic osrExitCountForReoptimization
budget (100) therefore leaves a newly hot previously-uncovered site
deoptimizing for ~101 calls.

This was observed downstream as oven-sh/bun#40477 on FrozenMiniSearch
scorePostingDoc (insert-only warmup, then update of existing Map
entries). Standalone JSC reproduces 101 same-site InadequateCoverage
exits at scorePostingDoc bc#77; with this patch the same site
reoptimizes on the 6th hit. Disabling the new option on the same
binary restores the 101-exit path. That is a heuristic change: under
normal FTL+concurrent JSC the wall-time of that repro is not
measurably improved.

Count repeats of that OSR exit's m_count against a dedicated
osrExitCountForReoptimizationFromInadequateCoverage threshold (default
5, same unsigned `count > threshold` convention and
adjustedExitCountThreshold / retry doubling as the existing counters).
Distinct sites keep independent counters so they do not share this
budget.

Default 5 is conservative: a durable phase change is indistinguishable
from t=3/4/6, while t=5 avoids jettison on a 5-then-never burst and
on interleaved 32-site x4/x5 sprays. m_count is cumulative, so any
fixed constant eventually fires on rare spaced hits; 5 buys two extra
lifetime hits of slack versus 3. This is not justified by FromLoop's
historical 5.

The new helper applies codeTypeThresholdMultiplier() like the generic
and FromLoop helpers (Eval x10, Function/Program x1). There is no
reason to diverge.

The option is dedicated rather than reused from
osrExitCountForReoptimizationFromLoop so that tuning the loop-stuck
budget does not silently change non-loop InadequateCoverage policy.
